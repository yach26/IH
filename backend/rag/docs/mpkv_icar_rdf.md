# MPKV-ICAR Fertilizer Recommendation Document — Kolhapur Region (2022)

## Introduction

This document provides fertilizer dose recommendations for major kharif and rabi crops grown in the Kolhapur district of Maharashtra, India.
Recommendations are based on multi-season trials conducted by Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth (MPKV) Rahuri and validated by ICAR.
All quantities represent total seasonal dose (kg per hectare) unless otherwise stated.

---

## Rice — Kolhapur District

**crop**: rice
**region**: kolhapur
**soil_type**: black, laterite
**document_type**: rdf

### Basal Dose (at transplanting)

For rice grown in Kolhapur on medium-to-heavy black soils, the recommended total seasonal dose is:
- Nitrogen (N): 100 kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 50 kg/ha
- Potassium (K2O): 50 kg/ha

Application schedule:
- Apply full P2O5 (50 kg/ha) as basal using DAP or SSP before transplanting.
- Split nitrogen: 50% N as basal, 25% N at tillering (21-28 days after transplanting), 25% N at panicle initiation.
- Apply full K2O at basal or split 50% basal + 50% at tillering.

### Tillering Stage Application — Rice

**crop**: rice
**crop_stage**: tillering
**region**: kolhapur
**nutrient**: N

At the tillering stage (21-28 DAT), apply the second split of nitrogen fertilizer.
Recommended: 25 kg N/ha using Urea (46% N) — equivalent to approximately 54 kg Urea/ha.
Avoid application if heavy rainfall (>30 mm/day) is forecast within 48 hours due to leaching risk.
Condition soil moisture to field capacity before application for optimum uptake.

### Panicle Initiation Stage — Rice

**crop**: rice
**crop_stage**: panicle_initiation
**region**: kolhapur
**nutrient**: N

At panicle initiation (45-55 DAT), apply the final split dose of nitrogen.
Recommended: 25 kg N/ha as Urea.
If yellowing observed before this stage, bring forward application by 5-7 days.

## Sugarcane — Kolhapur District

**crop**: sugarcane
**region**: kolhapur
**soil_type**: black

### Seasonal Dose

- Nitrogen (N): 250 kg/ha per crop cycle (12-15 months)
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 85 kg/ha
- Potassium (K2O): 115 kg/ha

Application schedule:
- Basal: 85 kg P2O5 + 25 kg N/ha at planting using DAP.
- Tillering (60-90 days): 75 kg N/ha using Urea or Ammonium Sulphate.
- Grand growth phase (120-150 days): 100 kg N/ha + 115 kg K2O/ha using Urea + MOP.
- Maturation (240-270 days): 50 kg N/ha, reduce K to 0.

Sugarcane in Kolhapur benefits from split potassium application due to the clay-dominant black soil's moderate leaching potential.

## Soybean — Kolhapur District

**crop**: soybean
**region**: kolhapur
**crop_stage**: sowing
**soil_type**: black

### Seasonal Dose

Soybean is a leguminous crop that fixes atmospheric nitrogen.
- Nitrogen (N): 25 kg/ha (starter dose only — basal)
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 50 kg/ha
- Potassium (K2O): 50 kg/ha

All doses applied as basal before sowing. No top-dress N required under normal rhizobium inoculation.
If soil test indicates pH < 6.0, apply 2 t/ha lime 15 days before sowing.

## Wheat — Kolhapur District (Rabi)

**crop**: wheat
**region**: kolhapur
**crop_stage**: basal
**soil_type**: black

### Seasonal Dose

- Nitrogen (N): 120 kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60 kg/ha
- Potassium (K2O): 40 kg/ha

Application schedule:
- Basal: Full P2O5 (60 kg/ha) + 60 kg N/ha at sowing using DAP + Urea.
- Crown root initiation (20-25 days): 30 kg N/ha using Urea.
- Jointing stage (40-45 days): 30 kg N/ha using Urea.
- Full K2O at basal.

## General Agronomic Notes — Kolhapur

1. Soil testing before planting is mandatory for reliable recommendations.
   Use DTPA-extractable Zn and Fe for micronutrient assessment.
2. Black soils in Kolhapur have high K-fixing capacity; K application must not be reduced even when soil K is moderate.
3. pH-adjusted recommendations: For soil pH > 8.0, reduce P2O5 by 10-15% and monitor Mn availability.
4. For pH < 5.5, apply lime and expect reduced N availability until pH correction.
5. Avoid broadcasting Urea at high temperatures (>35 degrees C) — ammonia volatilisation losses can reach 25-30%.
