# MPKV-ICAR Fertilizer Recommendation Document — Jalgaon Region (2021)

## Introduction

This document provides fertilizer dose recommendations for major crops in Jalgaon district, Maharashtra.
Jalgaon is known primarily as a banana-growing belt with alluvial and medium black soils.
All values are kg per hectare for a full crop cycle unless noted.

---

## Banana — Jalgaon District

**crop**: banana
**region**: jalgaon
**soil_type**: alluvial, black
**document_type**: rdf

### Seasonal Dose

Banana has a long growth cycle (10-14 months). Fertilizer application is split across 4-5 intervals.

Total seasonal dose:
- Nitrogen (N): 200 g/plant/year — reference plant density 2,000-2,500 plants/ha (use exact field density for kg/ha conversion).
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60-70 g/plant/year
- Potassium (K2O): 200-250 g/plant/year

**Note on density**: For fields where plant density is not recorded, MPKV recommends using mid-point 2,250 plants/ha as a provisional value. This must be flagged as a derived assumption in any recommendation that uses it.

Application schedule:
- Month 2: 50 g N + 30 g P2O5 + 50 g K2O per plant at rhizome establishment.
- Month 4: 50 g N + 75 g K2O per plant at vegetative growth.
- Month 6: 50 g N + 75 g K2O per plant at bunch development initiation.
- Month 8: 50 g N + 50 g K2O per plant at shooting stage.

### Banana Micronutrient Notes

**crop**: banana
**region**: jalgaon
**nutrient**: Zn, B

Banana in Jalgaon's alluvial soils is often deficient in Zinc and Boron.
Apply 25 kg ZnSO4/ha at planting and 2 kg Borax/ha at month 4 if leaf tissue analysis confirms deficiency.
Do not apply boron without confirmation — excess boron causes leaf scorch in banana.

## Cotton — Jalgaon District

**crop**: cotton
**region**: jalgaon
**soil_type**: black
**crop_stage**: basal

### Seasonal Dose

Cotton in Jalgaon is grown on deep black (vertisol) soils. Recommended dose (Bt hybrid):
- Nitrogen (N): 120-150 kg/ha (use 135 kg/ha as mid-point when range is given)
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60 kg/ha
- Potassium (K2O): 60 kg/ha

Application schedule:
- Basal: 60 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O + 45 kg N/ha at sowing.
- Squaring stage (45-50 days): 45 kg N/ha + 20 kg K2O/ha.
- Boll development (75-90 days): 45 kg N/ha.

Cotton on heavy black soils: avoid over-irrigation post-boll stage to reduce boll rot incidence.

### Cotton Tillering (Squaring) Stage

**crop**: cotton
**crop_stage**: squaring
**region**: jalgaon
**nutrient**: N, K

At the squaring stage (45-50 days after sowing), apply 45 kg N/ha using Urea and 20 kg K2O/ha using MOP.
This dose supports boll set and is critical for yield formation.
If rainfall has been excessive (>200 mm cumulative in 30 days), add 10% to compensate leaching losses.

## Maize — Jalgaon District (Kharif)

**crop**: maize
**region**: jalgaon
**soil_type**: black, red
**crop_stage**: basal

### Seasonal Dose

- Nitrogen (N): 120 kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60 kg/ha
- Potassium (K2O): 40 kg/ha

Application schedule:
- Basal: 60 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O + 40 kg N/ha at sowing.
- Knee-high stage (V6, 25-30 days): 40 kg N/ha using Urea.
- Tassel initiation (V10-V12, 45 days): 40 kg N/ha using Urea.

---

## Onion — Jalgaon District (Rabi)

**crop**: onion
**region**: jalgaon
**soil_type**: black, sandy-loam

### Seasonal Dose

- Nitrogen (N): 100 kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 50 kg/ha
- Potassium (K2O): 50 kg/ha

Application schedule:
- Basal: 50 kg P2O5 + 25 kg K2O + 40 kg N/ha at transplanting.
- 30 days after transplanting: 30 kg N/ha + 25 kg K2O/ha.
- 60 days after transplanting: 30 kg N/ha.

## General Agronomic Notes — Jalgaon

1. Jalgaon summers are extreme (>42 degrees C). Apply fertilizer early morning or evening during peak summer.
2. Avoid surface broadcasting of Urea in high-temperature conditions — use drip fertigation where infrastructure allows.
3. Banana fields with drip irrigation should use fertigated K2O in weekly splits during bunch development.
4. For black cotton soils: avoid waterlogging during early boll stage.
